# AI_USAGE_NOTES

AI assistance was used selectively for development support, design discussion,
debugging, and documentation. The final implementation was reviewed, tested,
and validated by me.


Tools used: ChatGPT and Claude (Anthropic)

1. seed_brand_abr.csv

---

I authored the ABR enrichment seed as a hand-maintained CSV mapping fuel
brands to placeholder ABNs, entity names, and confidence tiers.

No live ABR Lookup GUID/API was available within the assessment timebox.
The ABN values were based on general background knowledge and were NOT
verified against the ABR register. They may therefore be inaccurate.

The seed explicitly includes:
is_verified_via_live_abr_api = FALSE

This makes the enrichment limitation visible in the data itself.

2. nsw_fuel_extract.py

---

The Python extractor was created with AI assistance and includes OAuth2
client-credentials authentication, required API headers, calls to the six
NSW Fuel API endpoints, and output handling.

I ran the extractor myself using the real API credentials and used the
resulting stations.csv and prices.csv to build the dbt staging models.

The engineering decisions around the extracted data — including identifying
station code as the business key and recognising lastupdated as an intraday
timestamp — were reviewed and determined as part of my implementation.

3. dbt modelling

---

AI assistance was used while designing and reviewing the dimensional model,
including:

* Defining the fact table grain as station x fuel type x day.
* Structuring the fact and dimension tables.
* Designing dim_station, dim_fuel_type, and dim_date.
* Reviewing surrogate-key generation and fact-to-dimension relationships.
* Discussing staging, intermediate, Gold/mart, and reporting layer
  responsibilities.
* Reviewing the SCD Type 2 station snapshot approach.
* Suggesting business-focused reporting models such as daily price summary,
  price movement, station coverage, and cheapest station reporting.

The final model structure, SQL, assumptions, and implementation were reviewed
and validated by me.

4. dbt macros

---

AI assistance was also used while writing and reviewing reusable dbt macros,
including:

* cents_to_dollars
* generate_surrogate_key
* generate_schema_name

The final macros were reviewed and tested as part of the project.

5. Debugging and validation

---

AI assistance was used to help diagnose dbt and Snowflake errors during
development, including SQL compilation errors and relationship-test failures.

The fixes were applied and validated by running dbt builds and tests.
For example, a station-key relationship failure led to investigation of
station-code standardisation before surrogate-key generation.

6. Documentation

---

AI assistance helped structure and improve the project README, including
documentation of:

* Project architecture
* Data flow
* Layer responsibilities
* Fact grain
* Modelling decisions
* SCD2 approach
* Reporting models
* Testing
* Materialisation choices
* Assumptions and limitations
* Production considerations

AI assistance was also used to help prepare the pull request description
and supporting project documentation.

## Final responsibility

AI assistance was used as a development and review aid. I made the final
technical decisions, ran the code, validated the outputs, investigated
failures, and am responsible for the submitted implementation.
